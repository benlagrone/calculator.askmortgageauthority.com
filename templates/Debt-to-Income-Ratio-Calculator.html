Debt-to-Income Ratio Calculator

Estimate your front-end and back-end debt-to-income ratios so you can see how your income and monthly obligations compare to common mortgage guidelines.

Income and housing payment

Use your gross monthly income before taxes and the full monthly housing payment you expect a lender to count.

Include principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA dues, and mortgage insurance if those apply.

Other recurring monthly debts

Add only debts a lender typically counts, such as minimum payments on installment or revolving accounts.

Most lenders do not count utilities, groceries, phone bills, or insurance premiums that are not tied to the property.
