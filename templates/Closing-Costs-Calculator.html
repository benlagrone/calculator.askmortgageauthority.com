Lender and settlement fees

These are the fixed charges typically paid to the lender, title company, escrow agent, and local recording office.

Prepaids and reserves

These are the upfront tax and insurance amounts often collected at closing for escrow setup and initial coverage.

Many lenders collect a few months of taxes into escrow at closing.

Homeowners insurance is often collected for the first year at closing, especially on purchases.
